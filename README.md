## 👋 About me

I'm a **Software Architect and Tech Lead** with 10+ years of experience building cloud-native backend systems in **Go** and **Java**. I design architectures that stay simple to change: **hexagonal architecture, event-driven design, microservices and serverless on AWS**, backed by a strong testing culture (**TDD, BDD, DDD**).

I've worked on high-impact systems at **Globant**, **MercadoLibre** and **MAS Global**, where I lead technical decisions, mentor engineers and help teams ship reliably. I also run **Efezzor Soft**, my own software company, where I take products from idea to production.

I'm **Anthropic Certified (Architect, Foundations)** and I apply generative AI in two ways: as a building block inside products (LLMs, prompt engineering, agentic workflows) and as a multiplier in the engineering lifecycle.

## 🧱 How I approach software

- **Clean boundaries first.** Domain logic independent from frameworks, databases and cloud services.
- **Events over coupling.** Services that communicate through events stay easier to scale and evolve.
- **Tests as design feedback.** TDD and BDD shape the design, and the AAA pattern keeps tests readable.
- **Observable by default.** Metrics, logs and traces are part of the feature, not an afterthought.
- **Decisions in writing.** Trade-offs recorded so the team understands the *why*, not only the *what*.
